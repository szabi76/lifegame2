📖 Advanced Game of Life

Complete Game Guide

🎮 Conway's Basic Rules

The foundation of cellular automata:

  • Birth: A dead cell with exactly 3 neighbors becomes alive
  • Survival: A live cell with 2-3 neighbors survives
  • Death: All other cells die from loneliness or overcrowding

🖱 Basic Controls

  • Click/Drag: Draw cells on the canvas
  • ▶ Play: Start/pause the simulation
  • ⏭ Step: Advance one generation
  • 🎲 Random: Fill with random cells
  • 🗑 Clear: Remove all cells

🧬 Cell Types & Mechanics

🦠 Plague Cells (Red Circles)

Infectious disease that spreads through the population.

  • Consume 2× resources (0.02 vs 0.01 per generation)
  • Spread to neighbors with 25% infection chance
  • Die after age 5 with 40% mortality chance
  • Automatically infect cells born in chaos zones
  • Trigger: Click 🦠 Infect button (affects 25% of living cells)

⭐ Superbreed Cells (Gold Diamonds)

Enhanced survival and reproduction abilities.

  • Survive with 1-4 neighbors (instead of 2-3)
  • Can birth new cells with only 2 neighbors
  • 30% chance to pass trait to offspring
  • Trigger: Click ⭐ Enhance button (affects 20% of living cells)

✨ Cleaner Cells (Cyan Pulsating)

Restore order by removing special properties.

  • Convert neighbors back to normal cells
  • Emit sparkle particles (5% chance per generation)
  • 30% chance to spread cleaner trait to offspring
  • Remove plague, superbreed, and mutation effects
  • Trigger: Click ✨ Deploy button (affects 15% of living cells)

⚡ Advanced Systems

🧬 Mutation System

Random evolution creates hybrid super-cells.

  • 5% default mutation rate (adjustable 0-20%)
  • Mutated cells are purple hexagons
  • Gain superbreed survival (1-4 neighbors)
  • Keep original specialty (plague/superbreed)
  • Can be removed by cleaner cells

⚡ Resource/Energy System

Environmental constraints limit growth.

  • Each cell location starts at 100% resources
  • Living cells consume energy per generation
  • Resources regenerate slowly at 0.5% per generation
  • Low resources (<30%) = 30% increased death chance
  • Birth requires >20% resources available
  • Monitor: ⚡ indicator shows average resources

💥 Catastrophe System

Disasters create infection zones with lasting effects.

  • Random impact radius of 10-20 cells
  • Kills all cells in blast zone instantly
  • Creates pulsating neon chaos marker
  • Cells born in chaos zones are auto-plagued
  • Chaos intensity decays at 0.5 per generation
  • Spawns 30 colorful explosion particles
  • Trigger: Click 💥 Destroy button

🧪 Reaction-Diffusion Chemistry

Gray-Scott chemical system creates organic patterns.

  • Two chemicals: A (nutrients) and B (toxins)
  • Chemical A increases resources, B decreases them
  • Living cells consume A and produce B
  • Updates every 2 frames for performance
  • 5 presets: Coral, Mitosis, Waves, Maze, Fingerprint
  • Adjustable feed rate, kill rate, and diffusion
  • Blends with cellular automata layer

🎨 Visual Guide & Effects

Cell Shape Reference

  • ⬜ Square: Normal healthy cells
  • ⭕ Circle: Plague-infected cells
  • ◆ Diamond: Superbreed enhanced cells
  • ⬡ Hexagon: Mutated hybrid cells
  • ◉ Pulsating Circle: Cleaner cells

✨ Particle Effects

  • Explosion: Colorful bursts (magenta, pink, red, orange) from catastrophes
  • Sparkle: Cyan twinkles from cleaner cells
  • Death: Gray particles from destroyed cells

📊 Visualization Tools

  • Timeline Graph: Tracks population history for all cell types (100 generations)
  • Bar Chart: Real-time pulsating bars showing current cell type distribution
  • Statistics: Generation count, population, and cell type breakdowns

🎨 Color Themes

Choose from 5 themes: Neon (default), Ocean, Sunset, Matrix, Fire

📐 Preset Patterns

Classic patterns: Glider, Pulsar, LWSS, Gosper Gun, Pentadecathlon, Acorn
