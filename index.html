🎮 Basic Rules

Conway's Game of Life: cells live or die based on neighbors.

  • Birth: Dead cell with 3 neighbors becomes alive
  • Survival: Live cell with 2-3 neighbors survives
  • Death: Otherwise, cell dies

🦠 Plague Cells (Red Circles)

Infectious disease that spreads and kills.

  • Consume 2x resources (0.02 vs 0.01)
  • Spread to neighbors (25% chance)
  • Die after age 5 (40% chance)
  • Born in chaos zones automatically

⭐ Superbreed Cells (Gold Diamonds)

Enhanced survival and reproduction.

  • Survive with 1-4 neighbors (vs 2-3)
  • Can birth with only 2 neighbors
  • 30% chance to pass trait to offspring

✨ Cleaner Cells (Cyan Pulsating)

Restore order and remove specialties.

  • Convert neighbors to normal cells
  • Emit sparkle particles (5% chance)
  • 30% chance to spread to offspring
  • Remove plague/superbreed/mutation

🧬 Mutated Cells (Purple Hexagons)

Hybrid cells with enhanced abilities.

  • Random mutation (5% default rate)
  • Survive with 1-4 neighbors
  • Keep original specialty (plague/super)
  • Cleaner cells remove mutations

⚡ Resource System

Energy limits growth and survival.

  • Each cell starts at 100% resources
  • Living cells consume per generation
  • Resources regenerate slowly (0.5%/gen)
  • Low resources (<30%) = 30% death penalty
  • Birth requires >20% resources

💥 Catastrophe System

Disasters create infection zones.

  • Random radius 10-20 cells
  • Kills all cells in impact zone
  • Creates chaos marker (neon glow)
  • Cells born in chaos = auto-plagued
  • Chaos decays slowly (0.5/gen)
  • 30 explosion particles

🎨 Cell Shapes

  • ⬜ Square: Normal cells
  • ⭕ Circle: Plague cells
  • ◆ Diamond: Superbreed cells
  • ⬡ Hexagon: Mutated cells
  • ◉ Pulsating Circle: Cleaner cells

✨ Particle Effects

  • Explosion: Colorful bursts from catastrophes
  • Sparkle: Cyan twinkles from cleaners
  • Death: Gray fade from destroyed cells
